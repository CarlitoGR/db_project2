Q: What is COSC 631 about?
COSC 631 (Database Information Systems II), 3 credits, Spring 2022
  Instructor:   Dr. Bo Yang
  Prerequisite: COSC 531
  Source:       COSC_631_Syllabus_Spring_2022.pdf
  To study the further database techniques beyond which covered in the COSC531, and thus to acquaint
  the students with some relatively advanced issues. At the end of the course students should be able
  to: gain an awareness of the advanced issues in relational data models, learn about the query
  optimization technology and applications, familiarize with the XML, data-warehousing and data-mining
  techniques and other advanced topics, apply the knowledge acquired to solve simple problems.
